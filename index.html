Privacy policy

What we store

Your ratings, focus areas, and saved check-ins stay in your browser’s local storage on this device. We do not run user accounts, and we do not upload your check-in answers to a HAI server.

What we don’t collect

  • No account email or password for check-ins
  • No sale of personal wellness data
  • No tracking of your 1–10 scores on our servers

Feedback

If you send feedback, whatever you include in that message (such as an email address) is only used to understand and reply to your note.

Clearing your data

You can clear site data in your browser settings to remove saved check-ins from this device.
